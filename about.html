Founded in 2024, Stackly was born from a simple idea: to connect passionate explorers with the world's most exceptional spaces and guides, seamlessly.

Discover Stackly
OUR STORY

A quiet obsession with the details

What began as a shared address book among a handful of hoteliers has grown into a platform trusted by travelers in more than 50 countries. Every stay, every guide and every itinerary is still chosen the same way — by hand, with care.

2015
Stackly founded in Zurich with a small circle of hotelier friends.
2018
Launched curated experiences across 12 countries.
2021
Reached 100,000 travelers and opened our design studio in Kyoto.
2024
Named Travel Platform of the Year by three industry councils.

Our Mission

To unlock the world's most extraordinary spaces and connect modern explorers with authentic, unforgettable experiences through flawless technology and human care.

Our Vision

To become the global standard for premium travel, creating a world where every journey feels intimately tailor-made, effortless, and deeply inspiring.

Our Core Values

Uncompromising Curation

We don't do mass market. Every villa, chalet, and guide on Stackly is meticulously vetted, hand-selected, and tested by our internal team.

Frictionless Elegance

From the first click in our app to your flight back home, we engineer every touchpoint to be beautiful, intuitive, and completely stress-free.

Authentic Connection

Luxury means nothing without soul. We build deep, lasting relationships with local hosts to ensure you experience the true heartbeat of a destination.
